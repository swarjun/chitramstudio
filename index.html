Your all-in-one media toolbox — by Swarjun Tech

One app for your everyday media tasks

Chitram Studio brings the tools you reach for most into a single, fast, private app — no sign-up, and your files are processed right on your device.

What's inside

  • PDF Tools — merge, reorder, delete pages, arrange 2-up, add or remove a password, and redact.
  • Document Scanner — scan pages with your camera and save them as a clean PDF or images.
  • Photo Editor — crop, rotate, filters, adjust, draw, add text and stickers.
  • Slideshow Maker — turn photos and clips into a video with transitions and music.
  • File Compressor — shrink images and PDFs to save space.
  • Image to PDF — convert pictures into a single or separate PDFs.
  • Online Radio — stream thousands of internet stations with playlists and favorites.
  • Daily Fact — a fresh media fact every day.

Private by design

There's no account and no login. Your documents, photos and videos are processed on your device, and the files you create are saved to your own Documents folder. See our Privacy Policy for the details.
