Frequently asked questions

Where are the files I create saved?

Everything you create — PDFs, scans, edited photos, compressed files and videos — is saved into your device's Documents folder, in a sub-folder for each tool (for example PDF, Scans, Edited, Videos). Open a tool to see the list of files it has produced.

The document scanner camera won't open. What do I do?

The scanner needs camera access. When you first tap New Scan, allow the camera permission. If you declined earlier, enable it in your device Settings → Chitram Studio → Camera, then try again.

What can PDF Tools do?

Reorder or delete pages, arrange two pages per sheet (2-up), merge multiple PDFs, add or remove a password, and redact (permanently black out) sensitive content.

How do I make a slideshow video?

Open Slideshow Maker, add photos and clips, arrange them, pick a transition, size and photo fit, then tap Preview and save. Your video is exported to the Documents / Videos folder.

Does the photo editor keep my original photo?

Yes. Edits are applied to a copy and saved as a new file; your original image is never overwritten.

Why does Online Radio need the internet?

Radio streams are played live from the internet. Station listings come from the public Radio-Browser directory, and your favorites and playlists are stored on your device.

Is my data private?

There is no account and no login. Your documents, photos and videos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.
