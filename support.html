Frequently asked questions

Where are the files I create saved?

Everything you create is saved on your device in a ChitramStudio folder, with a sub-folder for each tool. Open a tool in the app to see the list of files it has produced.

DeviceWhere to look
Android PDFs: Documents/ChitramStudio/PdfTools
Scans: Documents/ChitramStudio/Scanner (PDF) and Pictures/ChitramStudio/Scanner (images)
Edited photos: Pictures/ChitramStudio/PhotoEditor
Videos and slideshows: Movies/ChitramStudio/VideoEditor
Screen recordings: Movies/ChitramStudio/Recordings
iPhone / iPadFiles app → On My iPhone (or On My iPad) → Chitram Studio → ChitramStudio
WindowsDocuments\ChitramStudio

The document scanner camera won't open. What do I do?

The scanner needs camera access. When you first start a scan, allow the camera permission. If you declined earlier, turn it on in your device settings, then try again:

  • Android: Settings → Apps → Chitram Studio → Permissions → Camera → Allow
  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → Chitram Studio → Camera
  • Windows: Settings → Privacy & security → Camera → let desktop apps access your camera

Menu names vary slightly between phone makers and versions.

What can PDF Tools do?

Reorder or delete pages, arrange two pages per sheet (2-up), merge multiple PDFs, add or remove a password, and redact (permanently black out) sensitive content.

How do I make a slideshow video?

On Android, open Video Studio and go to the Slideshow tab; on iPhone, iPad and Windows, open the Slideshow Maker. Add photos and clips, arrange them, pick a transition, size and photo fit, then tap Preview and save. See “Where are the files I create saved?” above for the folder.

Does the photo editor keep my original photo?

Yes. Edits are applied to a copy and saved as a new file; your original image is never overwritten.

Why does Online Radio need the internet?

Radio streams are played live from the internet. Station listings come from the public Radio-Browser directory, and your favorites and playlists are stored on your device.

Is my data private?

There is no account and no login. Your documents, photos and videos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.
