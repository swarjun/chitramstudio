Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Chitram Studio (“we”, “our”, “us”) is developed and operated by Swarjun Tech. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the Chitram Studio apps for Android (com.chitramstudio.toolbox), iPhone and iPad (“Chitram Studio - Media Tools” on the App Store) and Windows. Your use is also subject to our Terms of Service.

In short: Chitram Studio has no account or login. Your documents, photos, videos and recordings are processed on your device and stay there — we never collect, upload, sell or share your files. To keep the apps free and stable, we may use Google services that collect limited usage, crash and advertising data, as described below.

1. Information We Collect

Files you open or create

When you open, scan, edit, convert, compress or record something, the app reads that content only to perform the task you asked for and saves the result on your device. It is never sent to us.

Camera

Used to capture photos and scan documents into PDFs. Images are processed on your device; we do not collect or store camera data.

Microphone and screen capture

On Android, used only by the Screen Recorder, after you start a recording and approve Android's screen-capture prompt. On iOS, the microphone is requested by the video engine for video features. Recordings and videos are saved to your device and are not uploaded.

Storage and photo library

With your permission (“All files access” on Android, Photos access on iOS), the app reads and saves documents, images, videos, audio and PDFs so you can browse and work on them. All files stay on your device.

Analytics and crash data

We may use third-party services such as Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the apps are used and to fix crashes. These services may collect anonymous usage statistics (features opened, session length), crash and diagnostic reports (app version, device model, operating system version, the error), and device identifiers such as an app-instance ID. They do not receive your files, and we do not use this data to identify you.

If the Android app crashes, it may also offer to open your email app with a crash report addressed to crash.chitramstudio@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email.

Advertising

The apps may show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device’s advertising identifier, IP address and device information to show ads, limit repeats and measure performance, and may show personalized ads where the law allows and you have consented. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads). Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Information we do not collect

  • No account, name, email or password — there is no sign-up.
  • No precise location, contacts or call history.
  • No contents of your files, photos, videos, recordings or documents.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide the features you use, on your device.
  • To improve performance and stability, and to fix bugs and crashes.
  • To understand which features are used, so we can improve them.
  • To display ads that keep the apps free.
  • To answer messages you send to our support address.

4. Data Security

Your files remain on your device and are protected by its own security (screen lock, encryption). We do not store, upload or share your files or documents, and we do not sell, trade or rent user data to anyone. Data collected by the third-party services above is sent over encrypted connections. Settings such as theme, radio favorites and playlists are stored locally on your device.
