Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chitram Studio (the “App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles your information. Chitram Studio is a media toolbox — PDF tools, document scanner, photo editor, slideshow/video maker, file compressor, image-to-PDF and internet radio — developed and operated by Swarjun Tech. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here. Your use is also subject to our Terms of Service.

In short: Chitram Studio has no account and no login. Your documents, photos and videos are processed on your device, and the files you create are saved to your own Documents folder. We do not collect, upload, sell or share your content.

1. Information We Collect

a) Content you choose to open or create

When you pick a file, scan a document, or edit a photo/video, the App reads that content only to perform the task you requested and writes the result back to your device. This content stays on your device; it is not sent to us.

b) Information we do not collect

  • No account, name, email or password (there is no sign-up).
  • No location data.
  • No contacts.
  • No advertising profile of you built by us.

2. Permissions We Request

PermissionWhy we use it
CameraOnly for the Document Scanner, to capture pages. Images are processed on your device.
Photos / FilesTo let you pick the images, videos, audio or PDFs you want to work on, and to save your results.
InternetUsed by Online Radio to stream stations and load the public station directory, and to check for app updates.

You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings. The rest of the App keeps working without them; only the related feature is affected.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

The files you create are stored in your device's Documents folder (in a sub-folder per tool). Small preferences — such as your chosen theme, home layout, radio favorites and playlists — are stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to us.

4. Online Radio & Third-Party Content

The Online Radio feature plays live audio streams over the internet and lists stations from the public Radio-Browser directory (radio-browser.info). When you play a station, your device connects directly to that station's stream and to the Radio-Browser service; their handling of any connection data is governed by their own policies. We do not receive your listening history.

5. Analytics & Advertising

Chitram Studio does not include our own analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not build an advertising profile of you. App distribution platforms (Apple App Store, Google Play) may collect standard aggregate install and crash statistics under their own policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Security

Because your content is processed and stored on your own device, it is protected by your device's own security (lock screen, encryption). Please keep your device and operating system up to date.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “Last updated” date.

9. Contact Us
