Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Chitram Studio (“we”, “our”, “us”) is developed and operated by Swarjun Tech. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the Chitram Studio apps for Android (com.chitramstudio.toolbox) and Windows. Your use is also subject to our Terms of Service.

In short: Chitram Studio has no account, no advertising and no analytics. Your documents, photos, videos and recordings are processed on your device and stay there. We do not collect, upload, sell or share your files.

1. Information We Collect

Files you open or create

When you open, scan, edit, convert, compress or record something, the app reads that content only to perform the task you asked for and saves the result on your device. It is never sent to us.

Camera

Used to capture photos and scan documents into PDFs. Images are processed on your device; we do not collect or store camera data.

Microphone and screen capture (Android)

Used only by the Screen Recorder, after you start a recording and approve Android's screen-capture prompt. Recordings are saved to your device and are not uploaded.

Storage

With your permission (“All files access” on Android), the app reads and saves documents, images, videos, audio and PDFs so you can browse and work on them. All files stay on your device.

Crash reports

If the Android app crashes, it may offer to open your email app with a crash report addressed to crash.chitramstudio@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email. The report contains technical details (app version, device model, Android version and the error) and no files.

Information we do not collect

  • No account, name, email or password — there is no sign-up.
  • No location, contacts or call history.
  • No advertising identifier and no advertising or analytics profile.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide the features you use, on your device.
  • To fix bugs, using crash reports you choose to email us.
  • To answer messages you send to our support address.

4. Data Security

Your files remain on your device and are protected by its own security (screen lock, encryption). We do not store, upload or share your files or documents, and we do not sell, trade or rent user data to anyone. Settings such as theme, radio favorites and playlists are stored locally on your device.

Our apps are general-purpose utilities and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
